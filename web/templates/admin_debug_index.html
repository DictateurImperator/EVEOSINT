Reading these indices

Index = 100 × cost of the same quantities on the chosen date ÷ their cost on the reference date. Built-in baskets use published objects from the current SDE, with one unit per included item; this is not a market-volume-weighted or CCP index. A large reference-price item has more weight.

Source: locally imported zKill valuation quotes, not guaranteed market transactions. Each date uses the last positive quote available on or before that date. No future prices, kill-derived fallback prices or artificial 0.01 ISK replacements are used. Old carried quotes may hide later market changes.

Items with no positive quote by the reference date are listed and excluded for the entire curve. Earlier dates with incomplete coverage have no index. New ships are not inserted midway. Triglavians have their own family, including T2 variants; supercapitals stay separate from capitals. Capsules and special-edition market families are excluded from built-in ship baskets.
