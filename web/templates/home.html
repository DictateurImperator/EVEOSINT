0.1.3 DEV
Changes since 0.1.2

2D EVE Map

  • New 2D EVE map with historical sovereignty influence by Alliance or Coalition.
  • Economic mode highlights regional MER activity, relative to local production or by ISK value, with separate or combined indicators.
  • View a fixed economic period or play monthly changes with a consistent light scale, pause and a month slider.
  • Cinematic mode adds day-by-day Evolution playback and Changes comparison between two dates.
  • Consistent SOV colors make territorial changes easier to follow through history.
  • Drag to pan and zoom the map, including when Interactive mode is disabled.

Fight Heat Map

  • Visualize recorded combat activity across New Eden, with kill counts and ISK values where available.
  • Choose between Killboard, Total and Hidden data sources.
  • Filter combat by ship or ship class and by Alliance or Coalition, including attacker/victim and include/exclude rules.
  • Explore activity over a selected time range or play its evolution.
  • Click a highlighted system to open its killboard with the selected time range and filters.
  • Anoikis combat highlights and filtered killboard links now work with Interactive mode disabled.

Killboard

  • Switch between Known killmails, All MER losses (known and hidden), and Hidden losses without a matched killmail.
  • Filter by an entire ship or structure group, such as Frigate or Destroyer, on the main killboard and entity killboards, including ships, corporations and alliances.
  • Include or exclude groups for attackers, victims or either side, and combine them with individual ships and other filters.
  • Your filters are kept when switching data sources on the main killboard and can be restored from a bookmarked URL.
Base release: 0.1.2

Changes since 0.1.1

Improved graphs

  • SuperIntel Evolution graphs are now interactive, with hover details and zoom.
  • Ship Analysis graphs are now interactive, with hover details and zoom.
  • Coalition Population timelines have improved interaction and navigation.

History

  • Coalition logos are now displayed in affiliation histories when available.
  • Solar systems now have a SOV History view showing historical Alliance / Coalition ownership periods.
  • System SOV History also shows total kills in the system for each period and the corresponding share of recorded kill activity.
