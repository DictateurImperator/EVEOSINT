EVEOSINT uses a first-party cookie to remember your choice for up to six months. Essential cookies may also be used when you log in.

If you accept analytics, we record page views, approximate visitor countries and daily unique IP addresses. Audience identifiers use HMAC-SHA-256 with a secret key renewed each day. Raw IP addresses are not stored in the audience statistics. These statistics are kept on our server for up to 30 days and are not used for advertising.

Security and abuse prevention. If we detect abusive activity, we may retain the IP address involved and incident information to investigate the abuse and block further abusive access. Security records are kept for up to 30 days after the last incident; IP addresses with an active security block are retained while that block remains in place. This processing is based on our legitimate interest in protecting the service and applies even if you decline analytics.

You can decline analytics and continue using the site. No audience statistics are recorded for visits where you decline. You can change your choice at any time using the Privacy and security link.
