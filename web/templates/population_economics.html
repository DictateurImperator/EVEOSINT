Economics

Territorial estimates from the MER: regional NPC bounties, mining and production are apportioned by average daily sovereignty, then summed across regions. These are estimates of activity in held territory, including activity by other groups.

The regional share is mean systems held / mean total player sovereignty systems, using UTC day-end ownership throughout each month. Coalition membership is evaluated on each day. Average population uses the latest recorded population on or before each covered day. PvP counts membership at the time of the kill, including pilots who later left; active pilots must have participated as attackers in a PvP kill. Killmails and pilots are deduplicated across coalition members. NPC-only kills are excluded.
